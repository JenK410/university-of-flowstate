## Video

- Career videos, posters, and English WebVTT caption files are streamed from CareerOneStop.
- Career video library: https://www.careeronestop.org/Videos/CareerVideos/career-videos.aspx
- CareerOneStop is sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor.
- The selected career controls which official occupational video is shown.
- Every workforce training plan also loads the closest official occupational video with English audio and English WebVTT captions.
